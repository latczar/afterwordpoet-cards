# Theme: letting go. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I didn't stop caring.
I just stopped
leaving the light on.

Your hoodie went back
weeks ago.
The rest of you
is taking longer.

Letting go
was never one big moment.
It was a hundred small ones
I didn't tell anyone about.

I'm not over it.
I've stopped carrying it
into every room.

Your name came up
at dinner
and I kept eating.
That's how I knew.

I moved the furniture
so the room
stopped looking
like us.

Letting go isn't forgetting.
It's remembering
without reaching back.
