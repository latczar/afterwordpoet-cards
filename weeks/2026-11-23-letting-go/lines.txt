# Theme: letting go. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I didn't stop caring.
I just stopped
leaving the light on.

Your hoodie went back
weeks ago.
The rest of you
is taking longer.

Letting go
was never one big moment.
It was a hundred small ones
I didn't tell anyone about.

I'm not over it.
I've stopped carrying it
into every room.

Your name came up
at dinner
and I kept eating.
That's how I knew.

I moved the furniture
so the room
stopped looking
like us.

Letting go isn't forgetting.
It's remembering
without reaching back.

That song still
reminds me of you.
These days
I let it play.

Loving you
taught me
what I'll never
settle for again.

I hope they make you
happy.
I mean it
a bit more every week.

I never got
the closure.
So I made
my own.

Some love stories
end early
so the right one
has room.

We were good
for a season.
I'm allowed
to keep the good part.

One day
your name will just be
a name again.
I'm nearly there.
