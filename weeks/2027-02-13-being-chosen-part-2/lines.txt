# Theme: being chosen part 2. One quote a day.

I've been someone's maybe.
I'm ready to be
someone's yes.

You saved me a seat
without asking
if I was coming.

I don't need flowers.
I need you to
pick up
when I call.

You introduced me
as yours
like it was
the easiest thing.

Chosen feels like
being missed
before you've
even left.

I'm done
auditioning
for people
who already know me.

Someone out there
is hoping
for exactly
who you are.
