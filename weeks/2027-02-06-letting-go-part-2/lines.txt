# Theme: letting go part 2. One quote a day.

That song still
reminds me of you.

I let it play.

taught me
what I'll never
settle for again.

happy.

a bit more every week.

the closure.

my own.

end early
so the right one
has room.

We were good
for a season.
I'm allowed
to keep the good part.

One day
your name will just be
a name again.
I'm nearly there.
