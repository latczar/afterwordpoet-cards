# Theme: almost relationships. One quote a day.

We were never
together.
So why does it
feel like a breakup?

You called me
your person
but never
your partner.

Three months
of good mornings
and you still won't say
what we are.

I can't even
tell people
we broke up.
We were never anything.

You were
almost mine.
That's the part
that keeps me up.

Situationships give you
all the feelings
and none
of the rights.

I deserve
more than
'let's see
where it goes.'
