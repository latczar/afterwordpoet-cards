# Theme: almost relationships. First half posts everywhere; second half is the 1pm UK TikTok.

We were never
together.
So why does it
feel like a breakup?

You called me
your person
but never
your partner.

Three months
of good mornings
and you still won't say
what we are.

I can't even
tell people
we broke up.
We were never anything.

You were
almost mine.
That's the part
that keeps me up.

Situationships give you
all the feelings
and none
of the rights.

I deserve
more than
'let's see
where it goes.'

You texted 'wyd'
like it meant
something.
It didn't.

I memorised
your coffee order
for someone
who never claimed me.

Almost
is the loneliest
word I know.

You liked my story
but not enough
to stay in it.

I was
easy to talk to
and hard
to commit to.

I'm done
being someone's
plan B
on a Friday night.

Next time
I want a name
for what we are
from the start.
