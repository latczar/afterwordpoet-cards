# Theme: starting over. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

Starting over
isn't going back
to the beginning.
I know more now.

New year,
same me.
Just a bit
more honest.

I packed up the flat
and found I'd kept
so many things
I never even used.

You can start again
on a Wednesday.
It doesn't have to be
January.

I'm a beginner
at a lot of things
this year.
I'm letting that be fine.

The first step
was deleting
the old playlist.

I'm not who I was
when this started.
Good.
That was the point.
