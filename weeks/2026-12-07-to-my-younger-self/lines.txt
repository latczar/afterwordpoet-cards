# Theme: to my younger self. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

You'll stop
apologising
for taking up space.
It takes a while.

The people you're trying
to impress now
won't remember
any of it.

Wear the thing.
Nobody's looking
as hard
as you think.

Your mum was right
about some things.
Not all.
Some.

Being quiet
isn't the same
as being boring.

You don't have to
have it figured out
by twenty-five.
I still don't.

That song you're
embarrassed to love?
You still love it.
Good.
