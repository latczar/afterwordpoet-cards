# Theme: coming home for christmas. One quote a day.

Mum still makes
my favourite
without me asking.

My old bedroom
still smells like
being sixteen.

Coming home
is realising
your parents
got older too.

The house is loud,
the food is late,
and I wouldn't
change a thing.

Dad still asks
if I'm eating properly.
I'm a grown adult.
I still love it.

Christmas with family
is three days of love
and one argument
about the telly.

I came home
tired.
I'll leave
full.
