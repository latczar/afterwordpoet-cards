# Theme: coming home for christmas. First half posts everywhere; second half is the 1pm UK TikTok.

Mum still makes
my favourite
without me asking.

My old bedroom
still smells like
being sixteen.

Coming home
is realising
your parents
got older too.

The house is loud,
the food is late,
and I wouldn't
change a thing.

Dad still asks
if I'm eating properly.
I'm a grown adult.
I still love it.

Christmas with family
is three days of love
and one argument
about the telly.

I came home
tired.
I'll leave
full.

Nobody laughs
at my jokes
like my siblings do.
Mostly at me.

The empty chair
still gets
a cracker.

My nan
still slips me
a tenner
on the way out.

For one week a year
I get to be
somebody's kid again.

We watch
the same film
every year.
Nobody's tired of it.

If you can't go home
this year,
I hope someone
saves you a plate.

The best present
was everyone
in one room
for once.
