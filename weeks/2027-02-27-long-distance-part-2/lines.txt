# Theme: long distance part 2. One quote a day.

I hug my pillow
and pretend.
Don't tell anyone.

You sent me a photo
of the sky
so we'd be looking
at the same thing.

Distance didn't
make us weaker.
It made us
better at words.

I've memorised
your voice
at 2am.

One day
we'll argue about
the washing up.
I can't wait.

Every 'see you soon'
is a promise
I'm holding you to.

Missing you
is the price.
You're worth
every bit of it.
