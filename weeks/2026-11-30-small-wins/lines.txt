# Theme: small wins. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I answered the email
I'd been avoiding all week.
Nobody clapped.
I did.

Made my bed today.
It's not much.
It's a start
I can see.

I drank the water,
took the walk,
rang my mum back.
That's a good day.

Today I said no
without a long explanation.
Small thing.
Big for me.

I cooked a proper meal
for just me
and didn't feel
silly about it.

Some wins are quiet,
like sleeping through
the whole night
for once.

I went to the dentist
I'd been dreading
for two years.
Gold star for me.
