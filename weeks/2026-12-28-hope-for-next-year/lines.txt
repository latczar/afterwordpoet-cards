# Theme: hope for next year. One quote a day.

I don't need
a new me.
Just a kinder
version of this one.

Next year
I want fewer
almosts
and more definitelys.

I'm bringing
the lessons
into next year.
Not the grudges.

My only resolution:
stop shrinking
to fit
other people's plans.

I hope next year
someone texts
'home safe?'
and means it.

This year I learned
I can do hard things.
Next year,
easier ones please.

Same me,
new year,
better boundaries.
