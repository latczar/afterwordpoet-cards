# Theme: still hoping. One quote a day.

I still believe
someone's out there
getting ready
to meet me.

I haven't
given up on love.
I've just stopped
rushing it.

Still hoping
for the kind of love
I can bring
home to my mum.

My heart's been broken.
Still works.
Just checked.

I'm hopeful,
not desperate.
There's a difference.

One day
someone will
ask about my day
and wait for the answer.

I still
make wishes
on 11:11.
