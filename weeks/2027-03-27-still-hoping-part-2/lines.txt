# Theme: still hoping part 2. One quote a day.

The right person
is probably
having a bad day
too right now.

Hope is
buying two mugs
when you live
on your own.

I'm still
a romantic.
Just a careful one
now.

Every love song
still sounds
like a promise
to me.

I haven't met you yet,
but I hope
you're looking after
yourself.

I'm patient.
Mostly.
Okay,
I'm trying.

Still hoping.
Still here.
Still soft.
