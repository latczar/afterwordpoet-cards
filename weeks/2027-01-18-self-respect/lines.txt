# Theme: self-respect. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I stopped shrinking
to fit in rooms
that didn't want me
anyway.

I can be kind
and still say
that's not okay.

I left when
the jokes stopped
being jokes.

I'm not hard to love.
I'm just done
settling for
half of it.

If I have to beg,
it isn't mine.

I keep promises
to myself now.
Even the small ones.

I don't chase
people any more.
I walk beside
the ones who stay.
