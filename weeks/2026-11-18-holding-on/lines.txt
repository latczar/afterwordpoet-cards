# Theme: holding on. One quote a day.

Hope isn't loud.
Sometimes it's just
setting an alarm
for tomorrow.

I don't know
how it works out yet.
I just know
I'm still here for it.

One day
you'll tell this story
and it won't
hurt to say it.

The version of me
who cried in the car
would be proud
of me today.

Someone is going to
love the parts of you
you keep
apologising for.
