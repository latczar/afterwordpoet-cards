# Spare quotes, kept for next December. Not scheduled.

Nobody laughs
at my jokes
like my siblings do.
Mostly at me.

The empty chair
still gets
a cracker.

My nan
still slips me
a tenner
on the way out.

For one week a year
I get to be
somebody's kid again.

We watch
the same film
every year.
Nobody's tired of it.

If you can't go home
this year,
I hope someone
saves you a plate.

The best present
was everyone
in one room
for once.
