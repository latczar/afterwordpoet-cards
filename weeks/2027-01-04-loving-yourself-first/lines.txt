# Theme: loving yourself first. One quote a day.

I took myself
to dinner.
Great company,
no notes.

I'm learning
to talk to myself
like I'd talk
to my best friend.

Loving myself
looks like
going to bed
before 2am.

I stopped waiting
for someone
to buy me flowers.
The shop's right there.

I used to need
everyone to like me.
Now I just need
me to.

I'm not hard to love.
I'm just learning
to do it
myself first.

Saying no
without a long explanation
is my new
favourite thing.
