# Theme: long distance. First half posts everywhere; second half is the 1pm UK TikTok.

I know your time zone
better than
my own.

My goodnight
is your good morning.
I like that
you start the day with me.

We've had dinner together
on video call
more times
than I can count.

The airport
is my favourite place
and my least
favourite place.

I keep a list
of things to show you
when you're
finally here.

Long distance is
falling asleep
mid-sentence
on the phone.

Some days the miles
feel like nothing.
Some days
they're all I can feel.

I hug my pillow
and pretend.
Don't tell anyone.

You sent me a photo
of the sky
so we'd be looking
at the same thing.

Distance didn't
make us weaker.
It made us
better at words.

I've memorised
your voice
at 2am.

One day
we'll argue about
the washing up.
I can't wait.

Every 'see you soon'
is a promise
I'm holding you to.

Missing you
is the price.
You're worth
every bit of it.
