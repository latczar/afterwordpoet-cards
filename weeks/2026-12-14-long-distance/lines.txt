# Theme: long distance. One quote a day.

I know your time zone
better than
my own.

My goodnight
is your good morning.
I like that
you start the day with me.

We've had dinner together
on video call
more times
than I can count.

The airport
is my favourite place
and my least
favourite place.

I keep a list
of things to show you
when you're
finally here.

Long distance is
falling asleep
mid-sentence
on the phone.

Some days the miles
feel like nothing.
Some days
they're all I can feel.
