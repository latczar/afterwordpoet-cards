# Theme: the one that got away. One quote a day.

Some people
you don't get over.
You just
get used to it.

Wrong time,
right person.
Still hurts
the same.

I still wonder
what would've happened
if I'd said it
out loud.

I see someone
in your coat
and my heart
does a thing.

You're the 'what if'
I think about
in the shower.

I hope you think
of me
when that film
comes on.

I don't regret
loving you.
I regret
not saying so.
