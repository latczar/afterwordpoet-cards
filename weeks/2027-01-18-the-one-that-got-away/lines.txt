# Theme: the one that got away. First half posts everywhere; second half is the 1pm UK TikTok.

Some people
you don't get over.
You just
get used to it.

Wrong time,
right person.
Still hurts
the same.

I still wonder
what would've happened
if I'd said it
out loud.

I see someone
in your coat
and my heart
does a thing.

You're the 'what if'
I think about
in the shower.

I hope you think
of me
when that film
comes on.

I don't regret
loving you.
I regret
not saying so.

You got married.
I'm happy for you.
Mostly.

I measure
every new person
against
a memory of you.

We were young
and stupid
and I'd do it
all again.

Somewhere
you're living
the life
we talked about.

I didn't lose you.
I let you go
too easily.

I can still hear
your laugh
when I'm
somewhere quiet.

Maybe
in another life
I said
stay.
