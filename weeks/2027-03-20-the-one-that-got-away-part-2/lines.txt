# Theme: the one that got away part 2. One quote a day.

You got married.
I'm happy for you.
Mostly.

I measure
every new person
against
a memory of you.

We were young
and stupid
and I'd do it
all again.

Somewhere
you're living
the life
we talked about.

I didn't lose you.
I let you go
too easily.

I can still hear
your laugh
when I'm
somewhere quiet.

Maybe
in another life
I said
stay.
