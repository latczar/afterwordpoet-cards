# Theme: hope after heartbreak part 2. One quote a day.

One day
someone will say
they'll call
and actually call.

Healing looked like
sleeping in
the middle
of the bed.

I gave too much
to the wrong person.
I still have plenty
for the right one.

I still believe
in love.
I just believe
in me first now.

Some hearts
break open,
not apart.

I deleted the playlist.
Then I made
a better one.

Love didn't give up on me.
It was just
running late.
