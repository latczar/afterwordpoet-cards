# Theme: being loved quietly. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

You never said it much.
You just saved me
the last slice,
every time.

Some love sounds like
"text me
when you're home."

You remembered
how I take my tea.
I've had grand gestures
that meant less.

Quiet love is
someone moving their bag
so you can
sit down.

You didn't ask
what was wrong.
You just stayed
on the call.

I used to want fireworks.
Now I want someone
who notices
I'm tired.

The best people
love you in ways
that are easy
to miss.
