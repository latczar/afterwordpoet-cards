# Theme: when the holidays are hard. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

It's okay if this season
feels heavy.
Not everything bright
is easy.

There's an empty chair
at the table this year.
We keep setting
a place anyway.

Leave the party early
if you need to.
Rest is
a reason too.

Everyone's posting
their perfect Christmas.
Mine is quiet.
That's fine.

Some families
are hard to go home to.
You can love them
from a distance.

Grief gets louder
in December.
Turn the music up
anyway.

You don't have to
be merry.
Just be here.
That's plenty.
