# Theme: hope after heartbreak. One quote a day.

Your heart
will open again.
Not today, maybe.
But it will.

I thought I'd never
feel like this again.
Then I did.
Over a coffee.

Heartbreak taught me
where I end
and someone else
begins.

You'll laugh again
without checking
if it's allowed.

The first good morning
after a breakup
feels like
a small miracle.

I'm not looking
for someone new.
But I'm leaving
room for it.

It hurt
because it mattered.
It'll matter again,
with someone else.
