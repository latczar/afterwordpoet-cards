# Theme: missing someone. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I still reach
for my phone
to tell you things.

You'd have loved
this song.
I play it
for both of us.

Missing you
is quieter now.
It just turns up
at odd times.

Some days I forget
you're not around.
Then I remember
all over again.

I walked past
your favourite café.
It's a bakery now.
You'd hate that.

The hardest part
isn't the big days.
It's Tuesday afternoons.

I don't want
to stop missing you.
It means
you were here.
