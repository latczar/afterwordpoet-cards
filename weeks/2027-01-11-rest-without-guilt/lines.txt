# Theme: rest without guilt. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I took a nap
at three in the afternoon
and nothing
fell apart.

Rest isn't a prize
for burning out.
You can have it
before.

I cancelled plans
to stay in,
and for once
I didn't apologise.

My phone is on silent.
The world
can wait
an hour.

Doing nothing today
is still doing
something
for me.

You're allowed
to be tired
without a good reason.

I used to feel guilty
for resting.
Now I feel guilty
for not.
