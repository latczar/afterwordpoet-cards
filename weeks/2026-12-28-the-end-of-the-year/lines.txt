# Theme: the end of the year. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

I didn't do
half the things
I planned this year.
I did other things.

This year took more
than it gave.
I'm still here
to say so.

Before you count
what you didn't finish,
count what you
survived.

I'm not making
big resolutions.
Just fewer nights
pretending I'm fine.

Some of this year
I'd like back.
Most of it
I'm glad to leave.

Thank you to the me
from last January.
You had no idea
and showed up anyway.

The year is ending.
I'm not.
That's the part
that matters.
