# Theme: loving yourself first part 2. One quote a day.

I forgave
the version of me
who didn't
know better.

My body
carried me
through every bad day.
I'm done being mean to it.

I booked the trip
alone.
I was waiting for permission.
Turns out it was mine.

Self-love is
boring sometimes.
Water, sleep,
leaving early.

I'm somebody's
favourite person.
I'm trying
to be mine.

The love
I kept giving away
looks good
on me.

I'm not too much.
I'm a lot.
There's a difference.
