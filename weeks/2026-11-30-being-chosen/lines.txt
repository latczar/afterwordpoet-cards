# Theme: being chosen. One quote a day.

I don't want
to be someone's option.
I want to be
the obvious choice.

Being chosen
feels like
not having to ask
where we stand.

I want someone
who tells their friends
about me
before I meet them.

Choose me
on the boring days too.
That's where
life actually happens.

You never made me
compete for you.
That's when I knew.

I want a love
that makes plans
with me in them.

The right person
won't need
convincing.
