# Theme: late nights. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

It's 2am
and I'm rewriting
a conversation
from 2019.

Nothing good
gets decided
after midnight.
Go to sleep.

The ceiling
has heard more
of my thoughts
than anyone.

Night makes everything
sound louder
than it is.

I'm braver
at 1am
in my notes app
than in real life.

Some nights
you don't need answers.
Just a glass of water
and the window open.

Morning will make
this feel smaller.
It usually does.
