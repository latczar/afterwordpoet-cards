# Theme: friendships that faded. One card per block; line breaks are kept on the card.

We didn't fall out.
We just stopped
making plans
and called it busy.

I still have your number.
I just don't know
who'd pick up
any more.

Some friendships end
without a goodbye.
Just a last message
neither of us knew was last.

I hope your new friends
laugh at the same
stupid things
we did.

We were inseparable
at nineteen.
Now we like
each other's photos.

It's strange to miss someone
who isn't gone,
just somewhere
I'm not.

I'm not angry
we drifted.
I'm just glad
we were close once.
