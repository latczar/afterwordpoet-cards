# Theme: almost relationships part 2. One quote a day.

You texted 'wyd'
like it meant
something.
It didn't.

I memorised
your coffee order
for someone
who never claimed me.

Almost
is the loneliest
word I know.

You liked my story
but not enough
to stay in it.

I was
easy to talk to
and hard
to commit to.

I'm done
being someone's
plan B
on a Friday night.

Next time
I want a name
for what we are
from the start.
