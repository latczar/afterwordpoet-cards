# Theme: holding on part 2. One quote a day.

It's okay to rest
and still be
holding on.

Hold on
a little longer.
You'd hate to miss
what's coming.

I'm still hoping
for a love
that texts back
like it means it.

Some people
are worth the wait.
I'm hoping
one of them is you.

Tonight
it feels heavy.
Tomorrow
it'll feel a bit less.
